Group DH issuance runs in the runtime. A local group issues or receives a v2 DH credential through `runtime.operations.issuance`, the same live table, `issuanceStatus` and `waitForIssuance` as an individual, so a browser wallet with no CLI can act as the custodian or as a non-custodian member. New explicit calls (each may sign, none waits on a remote): `prepareGroupIssuance` (the custodian's first partial Grant and the proposal), `beginGroupIssuance` (an issuer member's Grant contribution and consent to its DH query contribution; the custodian may pass a credential instead of a proposal), `approveGroupIssuanceExchange` (a holder group member's consent) and `followGroupIssuanceProof` (verifies the custodian's final proof without signing). `groupIssuanceProposals(ops, group)` lists the stored Grant proposals a member may consent to and `groupIssuanceProof` exports the custodian's public proof. The owner's `processOnce` now performs the group steps: it sends each member's own proposal, authenticates the Grant, approves DH, sends the custodian's evidence, query and reply traffic, takes wrappers in, finishes the Grant, proposes the registry update through `proposeRegistryEvent` and broadcasts the proof to the other members. Root copies are sent once per member by the runtime's `RootReplicator`. A member that reloads reads `awaitingExchangeConsent` for a pending Grant and consents again with the same call; a custodian that reloaded after retaining its root reads `interrupted`, since the frozen coordinator and custodian are not stored. `IssuanceDeps` gains an optional `group` (the multiplexor and the registry proposal collaborators; the runtime composes it), `IssuanceDelivery.deliver` takes an optional `topic`, and `IssuancePhase` gains `awaitingGrantSignatures`.

`@keri-ts/tufa`: `tufa ipex --issue` and `tufa ipex setup` for a group are callers of these calls and keep their flags and output. The orchestration in `ipex-group-issuance.ts` is removed with no replacement: `acceptGroupIssuanceProof` and `awaitGroupIssuanceProof` become `acceptGroupDerivedProof`, `prepareGroupDerivedIssuance` no longer takes a wallet, and `--witness-gvrsn` no longer reaches the group path.
