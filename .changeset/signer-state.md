Signer availability is now state, read where it is needed, instead of a registry the runtime's loops wrote into. `Manager.hasSigner(pub)` says whether a signer is held or stored without decrypting or signing, and `Hab.canSign()` is a pure lookup over the keys `Hab.sign` would use (a group answers through its local member). Every background loop that signs reads it on its tock and skips a habitat that cannot sign yet through the skip path it already had: the mailbox poller neither polls nor backs off and polls within one tock of `Manager.hold`; a query request goes to the tail; a receipt or reply cue goes to the tail behind the cues that can go now (`Respondant.enqueueCue` returns a `CueOutcome`: `taken`, `unrouted` or `unsignable`); delegation keeps its row; Poster reports the forward as failed and delivers later events; Counselor and the registry-root replicator keep the copy without spending an attempt; the issuance, group IPEX and Unblind owners skip the operation until its signer is held (its Grant deadline still ends it); a registry root copy stays pending. `Habery.unsignable()` lists the accepted local habitats that cannot sign, with the keys they lack (`UnsignableHab`). Opening a store without a signer stays legal. A `SignerNotHeldError` that still escapes a loop is a fault and ends `runAgentRuntime`.

Breaking: removed `Manager.release`, `Manager.onHold`, `SignerConditions`, the `SignerCondition` type, `AgentRuntime.signerConditions`, the mailbox poller's `signerConditions` option and its `unsignable` `PollOutcome`. Held signers only grow while a store is open; `Habery.retireHab` drops the retired identifier's own through `Manager.forget(pre, current)`, which now returns `ForgottenKeys` (`deleted`, `released`), and `RetiredHab` carries `released`. Hosts that called `release()` after `close()` or after replacing a group member no longer need to. The wallet's `health()` arm is now `{ status: "signer-unavailable"; identifiers: readonly UnsignableHab[] }`, derived from `Habery.unsignable()` on each read, and sits below `mailbox-unreachable` in precedence.
