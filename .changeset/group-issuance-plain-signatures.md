Group DH issuance takes plain signatures and derives what it can. The group is derived from the Grant, the proposal or the credential's registry instead of being passed as a selection: `prepareGroupIssuance(ops, draft, coordinator?)`, the new `proposeGroupIssuance(ops, draft, options?)` (prepare, then the custodian's own approval in one consent), `approveGroupIssuance(ops, grant, options?)` (an issuer member's consent, replacing `beginGroupIssuance`), `approveGroupIssuanceExchange(ops, grant, coordinator?)`, `followGroupIssuanceProof(ops, grant, options?)` and `groupIssuanceProof(ops, grant)`. `groupIssuanceElection(ops, group, coordinator?)` is a pure read of the custodian a coordinator elects and whether the local member is it. The coordinator is an optional pin that defaults to the group's latest establishment event, and the custodian is always the election's: `--custodian` on `tufa ipex` is optional and only asserts it, and `--coordinator` is optional. `approveGroupIssuance` also refuses a proposal the elected custodian did not sign, so an election disagreement fails at consent instead of stalling DH. `GroupIssuanceSelection`, `BeginGroupIssuanceInput`, `beginGroupIssuance` and `GroupBegin` are removed with no replacement; `GroupIssuance` is built from a `GroupParty`, and `deriveGroupCustody` returns a `GroupElection`. The wallet's group `issue`, `proposals.approve`, `receive` and `unblind` call the new functions. keri-ts `AGENTS.md` gains the plain-signature and composition rules.
