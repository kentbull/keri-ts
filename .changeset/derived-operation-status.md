The runtime's operation owners keep an in-memory table of live operations only; their status is derived from the protocol stores. `IssuanceOperationStore`, `PresentationOperationStore`, `UnblindingOperationStore`, their `Memory...` implementations, the `IssuanceRecord`, `PresentationRecord` and `UnblindingRecord` types, the `store` dependency of every owner and the `operationStores.issuance`, `.presentation` and `.unblinding` options are removed with no replacement; backward compatibility with 2.1.0 is not kept. `issuanceStatus`, `presentationStatus` and `unblindingStatus` read the pending Grant and its deadline, the held registry root, the registry head, the accepted exchange, the accepted Admit and the peer's stored answer, so a reopened store reads what it read before, except for what lives only in memory: the transient phases, the `abandoned` and `failed` reasons, consent times and the destinations of a delivery. An issuer that ended between staging and anchoring reads `interrupted`; an explicit recovery action is planned for a later release. `resendPresentation(ops, said)` delivers a signed presentation again after a restart by rebuilding its wire from the stored signatures, without signing. The one remaining embedder port is `operationStores.derived`; it now defaults to `BaserDerivedUpdateStore`, which keeps the blind-derivation coordinates durably in the registry database (`dvus.`, a keri-ts-only sub-database). Wire formats are unchanged.
