Every runtime of a writable Habery now composes the per-identifier DH and Unblind owners (`runtime.ipex`), the operation owners (`runtime.operations`) and, unless `registryRoots: "refuse"`, the root replicator, with no option; a read-only Habery has `ipex === null`. `ipexOwners`, `ipexAuxiliary`, `AgentRuntime.ipexAuxiliary`, `IpexAuxiliarySelection`, `IpexAuxiliary` and the "actual local habitat" check are deleted outright, with no deprecated alias; backward compatibility with 2.1.0 is not kept. Obtain an owner with `runtime.ipex.dhke(hab)` or `runtime.ipex.unblind(hab)`. KRAM is still off when `kram` is omitted, as in KERIpy: the owners still compose, and `approve` of a DH or Unblind owner throws a "requires KRAM" error. `Dhke` and `Unblind` take an optional `kram` flag for this. The deprecated root-copy exports `RootCopyReport`, `RootResealFailure`, `RootCopyRecovery`, `replicateRegistryRoots` and `resealRegistryRoots` are removed; use `replicateRootCopies` and `resealRootCopies`, whose typed results the CLI maps to its unchanged `root_copy` lines. `openWebWallet` drops the `ipexOwners` and `ipexAuxiliary` entries of its `runtime` option, which narrows to `kram` and `operationStores`. Tufa's DH and Unblind commands take the identifier and use the runtime's owners; the individual Unblind command and the group issuance command no longer build a second replicator or operations table, so a group issuance sends exactly one root copy per member. `runPresentationUnblind` is replaced by `runIndividualUnblind` and `runGroupUnblind`. Wire formats are unchanged.
